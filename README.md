# Child Mortality Rate
This project analyzes child mortality rate of children under 5years cross continents Africa, Asia, Europe, North America, Oceania and South America between year 2000 to 2022.
### Step 1: Data Collection
* This dataset was provided by a friend.
### Step 2: Data Cleaning
* The dataset didn't require any cleaning to be done on it.
* I made sure that the data types were properly assigned.
### Step 3: Data Analysis
I made use of DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) in Power BI to calculate for average mortality rate.  
### Step 4: Data Visualization
The visualization was done using Power BI. I used area charts to show the mortality rates for the top five (5) countries for each of the continents depending on your selection. I used a map with gradient colours to indicate the mortality rate, a bar chart to show mortality rate for each year and a narative visual for a quick narration of the selected/filtered data.

### Key Insights
1. Mortality rate was at its peak in 2000 with an average of 5.46%
2. The top 5 countries in terms of mortality rate in Africa were Sierra Leone, Niger, Somalia, Chad, and Nigeria.
3. Top 5 countries in Asia were Afghanistan, Pakistan, East Timor, Laos, and Myanmar.
4. Top 5 countries in Europe include Kosovo, Moldova, Albania, Romania, and Ukraine.
5. Top 5 countries in North America were Haiti, Dominican Republic, Guatemala, Honduras, and Dominica.
6. Top 5 countires for Oceania were Kiribati, Papua New Guinea, Marshall Islands, Nauru, and Micronesia. 
7. Top 5 countries for South America include Bolivia, Guyana, Paraguay, Suriname, and Peru.
